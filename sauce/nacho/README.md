# Basic chedder sauce / Nacho!
See tips for variants

## Ingredients

| item             | quantity | qty(m) |
| ---------------  | -------- | ------ | 
| salt             | 1/4 tsp  | ~ 1g   | 
| butter           | 1.5 Tbsp |  20g   | 
| flour            | 2.5 Tbsp |  20g   |
| milk, warm       | 1 cup    | 244g   |
| cheddar, grated  | 5 oz     | 140g   |
| chili powder     | 1/4 tsp  | ~ 1g   |
| onion, minced    | 1/4 cup  |  40g   |
| jalapeño, minced | 1/4 cup  |  40g   |


## Cooking
1. Heat milk to 150ºF (65ºC)
1. Melt butter in saucepan
1. Add chili powder, and salt (and/or other spices)
1. Add onion and jalapeño, cook 3-4 minutes
1. Over medium heat, add flour and in stir out any lumps
1. Cook flour for about 3 minutes
1. Remove from heat until it stops sizzling
1. Add hot milk, incorporating in small amounts:
Whisking or spooning until smoooth, in about 8 installments will keep
the cheese sauce smooth. Return to low heat about 1/2 way.
1. Stir in more salt/spice to taste until warm and fully
incorporated. 

## Tips
- Keep the sauce under 175ºF / 80ºC at all times. 
This will keep the sauce from breaking. 
- This spicy, oniony version is what I call Nacho cheese but, 
skip the vegetables and replace the spicyness with paprika
and/or mustard powder for a more classic cheese sauce. 
You can also replace some or all of the milk with beer for a
cheddar beer dip!
