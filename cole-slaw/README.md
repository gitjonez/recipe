# Cole slaw

## Ingredients
- 1 small cabbage shredded and chopped (about 6 cups)
- 1 large carrot shredded
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- ⅓ cup granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 1 ½ teaspoon lemon juice fresh lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon salt more or less to taste
- ⅛ teaspoon pepper more or less to taste
  
## Instructions
1. Cut the cabbage in half and remove the core from each side.
1. Using a sharp knife cut the cabbage in very thin slices,
then chop the slices into small pieces.
Place the prepared cabbage in a large mixing bowl.
1. You can use a food processor to slice or shred the cabbage,
or you can also use a box grater to shred the cabbage and carrot.
1. Lightly salting shredded cabbage for 20 to 30 minutes draws
out excess moisture before the dressing is added.
This simple step helps prevent watery coleslaw,
especially if you're making it several hours ahead.
1. In a medium bowl, combine mayonnaise, sugar,
vinegar, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Mix until smooth and creamy.
1. Pour the slaw dressing over the cabbage and carrots.
Thoroughly mix to combine. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
1. Cole slaw is always better after sitting for a day,
make sure to mix the slaw a few times as it sits in the fridge.

## Notes
### Substitutions
- Replace apple cider vinegar with white vinegar, rice vinegar,
or fresh lemon juice for a slightly different tang.
- Swap granulated sugar for honey or maple syrup
if you prefer a natural sweetener.
- Use Greek yogurt in place of part of the mayonnaise
for a lighter dressing with extra protein.
- Add Dijon mustard for a little extra zip
and depth of flavor.
- Substitute pre-shredded coleslaw mix for freshly
shredded cabbage when you're short on time.

### Variations
- *Southern Coleslaw*: Use Duke's mayonnaise and increase the celery
seed slightly for a classic Southern barbecue flavor.
- *KFC-Style Coleslaw*: Finely chop the cabbage and carrots,
increase the sweetness a touch, and allow the slaw to
chill overnight for that familiar restaurant-style texture.
- *Carolina Vinegar Slaw*: Skip the mayonnaise entirely and toss the
cabbage with apple cider vinegar, a little sugar,
celery seed, and black pepper for a bright,
tangy slaw that's perfect on pulled pork.
- *Apple Coleslaw*: Fold in crisp diced Honeycrisp or Granny Smith apples
for a refreshing sweetness that pairs beautifully with pork and chicken.
- *Broccoli Slaw*: Replace the cabbage with shredded broccoli stems
for a hearty variation with extra crunch and nutrition.
- *Mexican Coleslaw*: Add chopped cilantro, sliced jalapeños, lime juice,
and a pinch of cumin. It's fantastic with tacos, fajitas, or grilled shrimp.
- *Bacon Ranch Coleslaw*: Stir crumbled bacon and a spoonful of ranch
seasoning into the dressing for a smoky,
crowd-pleasing variation that's perfect for cookouts.
